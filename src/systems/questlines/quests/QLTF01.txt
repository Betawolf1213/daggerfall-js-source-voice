-- The port's own script on DFU's machine (systems/questlines/qlLines.js). Started by the line's runner while the
-- Starfall runs (`needs: ['starfall']`), never offered by a questor: the letter is the offer, and a raven brings it.
-- Witnessed (net/questlineClaims.js `star`, the j1 receipt): it waits on a fallen star held (`ql act star-held 1`),
-- then on the player at the Glenmoril Coven (`permanent GlenmorilCoven`, the lycanthropy cure's own place - $CUREWER).
-- A coven is an open-air place in Daggerfall - one block of the wilds (WITCAA00.RMB), no building and no dungeon, so no
-- quest marker to stand a person on - and DFU's own quests meet one as this does, by the player's arrival (`pc at`,
-- S0000007's). Morwen meets the player at its fire. Lore: canon - the Witches' Festival on the 13th of Frostfall
-- (TEXT.RSC 8349), the music in the woods (Q0C4XY04.QRC: "The witches must be celebrating"), the Glenmoril Witches who
-- summon Hircine alone (FACTION.TXT 419, `summon: 4`), Mephala's summoning day the Festival's own
-- (systems/daedraSummoning.js). The port's own: the covens' rite that sings a star down and binds it in black thread,
-- Morwen of Glenmoril, her raven.

QBN:
Item _letter_ letter used 1011
Item _gold_ gold range 300 to 600

Place _glenmoril_ permanent GlenmorilCoven

Clock _courier_ 01:00 03:00
Clock _wait_ 60.00:00 0 flag 1 range 0 1

--	Quest start-up:
	pc at _glenmoril_ set _atcoven_ saying 1018
	start timer _courier_

variable _atcoven_

_courier_ task:
	give pc _letter_ notify 1012
	reveal _glenmoril_
	log 1010 step 0
	start timer _wait_

_held_ task:
	ql act star-held 1
	log 1015 step 1

_met_ task:
	when _held_ and _atcoven_
	say 1013
	say 1014
	give pc _gold_
	end quest

_wait_ task:
	say 1003
	end quest
